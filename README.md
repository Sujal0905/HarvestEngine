# AgroNex
Agriculture is one of the most important parts of our daily life, but farmers face many problems such as water shortage, high electricity costs, changing weather, and difficulty in choosing the right crop. Many farmers still depend mainly on their experience to decide when to water their crops or which crop to grow. Because of this, water and electricity can be wasted, and crop production may be affected.

Our idea is to build a **Smart Agriculture platform** that helps farmers make better decisions using data and AI/ML. The farmer can enter basic information such as location, crop type, soil type, and farm area. The system can also use weather information and, in the future, data from soil moisture and temperature sensors.

Based on this information, our system will suggest suitable crops and give irrigation recommendations. For example, if the soil has enough moisture or rain is expected soon, the system can suggest delaying irrigation. If the soil becomes too dry, it can alert the farmer that irrigation is needed. We also plan to use AI/ML to analyze the collected data and predict crop productivity and future water requirements.

Our main focus is not only on increasing crop production but also on **saving water and electricity**. By reducing unnecessary irrigation, farmers can use less water and reduce the running time of electric or diesel pumps. This can lower farming costs while making agriculture more sustainable.

The unique part of our idea is that we are bringing **water management, energy saving, crop selection, weather information, and AI-based recommendations together in one simple platform**. The system can start with available datasets and later be connected to real sensors on farms.

The main beneficiaries will be farmers, especially small and marginal farmers who may not have access to expensive smart farming systems. Our goal is to make technology simple and useful for farmers, helping them make better decisions, save resources, reduce costs, and improve productivity.
